AFRO Birmingham

Catch a vibe and a full belly at Afro Birmingham. A vibrant dining spot known for its authentic Nigerian cuisine and lively ambiance. Experience bold flavors, beautifully crafted dishes, and an atmosphere that feels like home.

AFRO! Restaurant & Lounge is a black-owned African restaurant and lounge celebrating culture, flavor, and togetherness. We invite you to join us for birthday specials, date night vibes, or your regular night out with friends. Our menu features authentic Nigerian dishes including Ayamase, Egusi, Efo Riro, Seafood Okro, Jollof Rice, and more.

With live music, expertly crafted cocktails, and an inviting interior, AFRO Birmingham is the perfect spot for a luxurious dining experience. Whether you're looking for halal eats or simply want to enjoy authentic African cuisine, we have something special waiting for you.
