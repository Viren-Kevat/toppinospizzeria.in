Our Story

Two Kitchens, One Fire

Why a Neapolitan pizzeria and a Turkish kitchen share the same brick oven.

Why Toppino's

A Relaunch Built Around the Oven

Toppino's is relaunching in Thaltej as a Turkish–Italian café built around one idea: a live brick oven, used for two traditions at once. On one side, hand-stretched Neapolitan pizza dough. On the other, boat-shaped Turkish pide.

It's a simple pairing that hasn't been done quite this way in the neighbourhood and it lets us keep the menu focused on a handful of things done carefully, rather than trying to be everything to everyone.
