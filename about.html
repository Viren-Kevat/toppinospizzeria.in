Our Story

Why Toppino's

A Relaunch Built Around the Oven

Toppino's is relaunching in Thaltej with an Italian-led menu anchored by Neapolitan pizza and pasta, alongside a broad mix of other favourites. The menu also has smaller selections of Turkish, Greek, Mexican and Mediterranean dishes.
