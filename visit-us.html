Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Toppino's located?

Toppino's is on Sindhu Bhavan Road, near Ornet Park, 3, Sindhu Bhavan Marg, PRL Colony, Thaltej, Ahmedabad 380059.

What are Toppino's opening hours?

Toppino's is open Monday to Sunday, 5:00 PM to 2:00 AM.

What kind of pizza does Toppino's serve?

We serve Neapolitan pizza, made with hand-stretched dough and baked in a live brick oven.

How much do pizzas cost?

Our Neapolitan pizzas start at ₹399. Pasta starts at ₹349 and salads at ₹325.

What else is on the menu?

Pasta, starters, Italian toasts, salads, soups, desserts, ice creams, mocktails, cold coffee and soft drinks.

Is Toppino's family friendly?

Yes. Toppino's is designed for family dinners, date nights and long tables of friends.
